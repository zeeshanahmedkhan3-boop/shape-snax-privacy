Privacy Policy for Shape Snax

Last updated: 8 October 2026

The short version: Shape Snax does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no internet connection. This applies equally to children and adults.

Who this policy is from

Shape Snax is developed and published by Zak's Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the Shape Snax mobile application handles information. Questions can be sent to zeeshan.ahmed.khan3@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect any personal or sensitive user data. Specifically, the app does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any contact details
  • Location data of any kind
  • Photos, camera, microphone, or file access
  • Contacts, calendar, or other device content
  • Advertising identifiers, device identifiers, or IP addresses
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or behavioural data

The app requests no Android runtime permissions and does not use an advertising ID.

Information stored on your device

Shape Snax saves two small settings locally on the device: your highest score, and whether sound is switched on or off. This information never leaves the device. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else, and it is not linked to any identity. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes it.

Advertising, purchases, and third parties

There is no advertising in Shape Snax. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions. We use no third-party SDKs, analytics providers, or advertising networks. No data is shared with or sold to any third party, because no data is collected.

Network access

Shape Snax functions entirely offline. It makes no network requests and contains no external links, no social media integrations, and no user-to-user communication features.

Children's privacy

Shape Snax is suitable for players of all ages, including children under 13. Because the app collects no data whatsoever, it does not knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information from children. It is designed to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) including provisions relating to children, and Google Play's Families Policy.

There is no login, no chat, no user-generated content, no advertising, and no way for a child to spend money or share information through the app.

Your rights

Data protection laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you. Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to retrieve, correct, or erase. To remove the locally stored high score and sound setting, uninstall the app.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how information is handled, we will update this page and revise the date above before releasing that version.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about Shape Snax can be sent to zeeshan.ahmed.khan3@gmail.com.
